Deploy

Put your project online — free

THANU AI doesn't host sites itself, but here's exactly how to launch anything you build on real, free hosting.

01 / PICK A HOST

Netlify or Vercel

Both have solid free tiers. Netlify is simplest for plain HTML/CSS/JS sites like this one.

02 / UPLOAD

Drag & drop your folder

On Netlify: sign in, go to "Add new site" → "Deploy manually", then drag in your project's public folder.

03 / GO LIVE

Get your free link

You'll get a link like yourname.netlify.app instantly. Share it with anyone.

Need a server too (for the AI chat backend)? Try Render.com — connect your GitHub repo, add your ANTHROPIC_API_KEY or GEMINI_API_KEY as an environment variable, and it deploys index.js automatically.
